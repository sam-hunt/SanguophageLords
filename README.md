## About

Biotech's lore paints sanguophages as ancient and secretive, eternal lords who hide among ordinary people. In play you mostly meet them in small numbers: a master and a few thralls in a crashed shuttle, or a handful of them asking to hold a meeting at your colony. This mod gives them a home of their own, a sanguophage lair deep below the ground, reached by an ancient stone stairwell.

## Features

### The lair

- **Ancient stairwell**: A stone stairwell on the surface leads down into the lair, and a second one at the bottom leads back up.
- **A new lair every time**: Each lair is laid out fresh around the same plan. An entrance hall leads to a columned processional and the master's deathrest sanctum, with tombs, crypts, a library, a dining hall, a prison, a gene laboratory, a vault and more around them.
- **Inhabitants**: An elder sanguophage, several younger sanguophages and a number of thralls. Some initially deathresting or alert and ready to call reinforcements or activate other countermeasures.
- **Loot**: Archite capsules and deathrest capacity serums in the vault, gene modification equipment in the laboratory, books in the library, an art gallery, tomb, and the lair's supply of hemogen.
- **DLC and mod rooms**: Royalty adds a throne room, Ideology a temple and Anomaly a ritual chamber. With Vanilla Races Expanded - Sanguophage, a draincasket room feeds hemogen to the deathrest chambers.

### Finding a lair

- **Odyssey landmarks**: With Odyssey, a lair entrance can appear on rocky landmarks (caverns, chasms, crevasses, hollows, cliffs, plateaus, valleys and basins) and, more rarely, on ancient ruins and quarries. The tile lists "sanguophage lair" among its features.
- **Planned**: A rare quest that marks a lair on the world map, so the lair can be found without Odyssey. Until it ships, Odyssey landmarks are the only way in, and the quest frequency slider in the mod settings has no effect.

## Requirements

- **RimWorld 1.6** or **1.7**
- **Biotech DLC** (required, since the lair is built on Biotech's sanguophages, deathrest and hemogen)
- **Odyssey DLC** (recommended, since its landmarks are currently the only way to find a lair)

## Installation

### Steam Workshop (Recommended)

Coming with the first release.

### Manual Installation

1. Download the latest release from the [Releases](https://github.com/sam-hunt/SanguophageLords/releases) page
2. Extract the `SanguophageLords` folder to your RimWorld `Mods` directory:
   - **Windows**: `C:\Program Files (x86)\Steam\steamapps\common\RimWorld\Mods\`
   - **Mac**: `~/Library/Application Support/Steam/steamapps/common/RimWorld/RimWorldMac.app/Mods/`
   - **Linux**: `~/.steam/steam/steamapps/common/RimWorld/Mods/`
3. Enable the mod in RimWorld's mod menu
4. Restart RimWorld

## Compatibility

- **Safe to add** to existing saves, but lairs are placed when a world is generated, so an existing world will not gain any.
- **Not safe to remove** from saves.
- Not tested with Combat Extended.
