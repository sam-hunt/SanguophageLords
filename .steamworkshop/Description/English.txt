Sanguophage Lords

[h1]About[/h1]
Biotech's lore paints sanguophages as ancient and secretive, eternal lords who hide among ordinary people. In play you mostly meet them in small numbers: a master and a few thralls in a crashed shuttle, or a handful of them asking to hold a meeting at your colony. This mod gives them a home of their own, a sanguophage lair deep below the ground, reached by an ancient stone stairwell.

[h2]The Lair[/h2]
[list]
[*]A stone stairwell on the surface leads down into the lair, which is laid out fresh every time around the master's deathrest sanctum
[*]An elder sanguophage, three lesser sanguophages and eight thralls live there, most of them asleep or in deathrest until you wake them
[*]The vault holds archite capsules and a deathrest capacity serum, and the gene laboratory, library, gallery and larder all have something worth taking
[*]Royalty adds a throne room, Ideology a temple and Anomaly a ritual chamber. With Vanilla Races Expanded - Sanguophage, a draincasket room feeds hemogen to the deathrest chambers
[/list]

[h2]Finding a lair[/h2]
With Odyssey, a lair entrance can appear on rocky landmarks such as caverns, chasms and cliffs, and more rarely on ancient ruins and quarries. Lairs are placed when a world is generated.

A rare quest that marks a lair on the world map without Odyssey is planned. Until it ships, Odyssey landmarks are the only way in.

[h1]Compatibility[/h1]
[list]
[*]Requires RimWorld 1.6 or 1.7 and the Biotech DLC. Odyssey is recommended.
[*]Safe to add to existing saves, but an existing world will not gain any lairs. Not safe to remove.
[*]Not tested with Combat Extended
[/list]
